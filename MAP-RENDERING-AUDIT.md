## What the three status words mean

- **Structural: pass**: the layout was decoded again from the ROM; both tilesets resolve; their tile art was found; no metatile id is outside its tileset; fewer than 2% of blocks point at a tile outside the art; the PNG exists, has exactly width×16 by height×16 pixels and is not one flat colour; its hash is the audited one.

- **Runtime-reference verified**: blocks of the image were matched pixel for pixel against an mGBA screenshot. Only the maps listed in `content/map-review.json` under `runtime`.

No map is claimed to be pixel-perfect beyond those statements.

## Limits of a static picture

- A picture is one still: animated tiles (water, flowers, waterfalls) show the frame stored in the tile sheet.
- No time-of-day tint, weather, fog or underwater blending: colours are the stored palettes.
- No people, item balls, berry trees, boulders or other objects, and no doors opened or tiles changed by scripts: the layout as stored in the ROM.
- Maps the game fills while you play (Battle Pyramid floors, Trainer Hill floors, Secret Bases) show only their stored template.
- Faraway Island Entrance and Southern Island (both maps) use the Rustboro secondary tileset in the game data (data/layouts/layouts.json), where Emerald uses other tilesets. The site shows what the ROM defines; whether these look right in play was not checked.
