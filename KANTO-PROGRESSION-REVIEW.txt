CHAPTER-BY-CHAPTER JOURNEY
The proposal was shown in chat and saved before website edits. Kanto chapters 1–15 are site chapters 20–34.

1 / site chapter 20: The S.S. Aqua — Olivine to Vermilion
Collect the S.S. Ticket, upgrade your Pokédex and sail to Kanto.
Next: Vermilion City. Straight off the ship.

2 / site chapter 21: Vermilion City — Lt. Surge · Level 57–60
Find the two hidden switches and beat Lt. Surge.
Next: Celadon City via Saffron. North along Route 6, then west from Saffron on Route 7. Save Sabrina’s Gym for later.

3 / site chapter 22: Celadon City — Via Saffron · Erika · Level 60–62
Cut into the Gym’s garden and beat Erika.
Next: Cerulean City & the Power Plant. Return east along Route 7 to Saffron, then north on Route 5.

4 / site chapter 23: Cerulean City — Power Plant, cape & Misty · Level 61–63
Recover the Power Plant’s missing part, fetch Misty from the cape and beat her.
Next: Lavender Town. Fly to Saffron and take Route 8 east for the Radio Upgrade; then continue toward Fuchsia.

5 / site chapter 24: Lavender Town — The Power Plant’s Radio Upgrade
Collect the Radio Upgrade, then follow the coastal routes south toward Fuchsia.
Next: Fuchsia City. Go south along Routes 12, 13, 14 and 15. Surf across Route 12’s water and use the eastern southern exit.

6 / site chapter 25: Fuchsia City — Routes 12–15 & Janine · Level 61–64
Follow the coastal routes from Lavender and earn Janine’s Soul Badge.
Next: Return to Saffron: Sabrina. Fly to the Saffron City you visited earlier. Its Gym is the next difficulty step.

7 / site chapter 26: Saffron Gym — Return for Sabrina · Level 64–66
Work through the teleporter maze to Sabrina.
Next: Snorlax & Diglett’s Cave. Fly to Vermilion with the Radio Upgrade already collected in Lavender.

8 / site chapter 27: Snorlax & Diglett’s Cave — Vermilion revisited · Opening western Kanto
Use the Poké Flute to wake Snorlax and cross to Route 2.
Next: Pewter City. Diglett’s Cave to Route 2, then north.

9 / site chapter 28: Pewter City — Brock · All six at Level 66
Cut through to Pewter and beat Brock.
Next: Viridian Forest, Viridian & Pallet. Walk south through Route 2 and Viridian Forest, then continue to Pallet on Route 1.

10 / site chapter 29: Viridian Forest & Pallet Town — Viridian City, Route 1 & Route 21
Travel south from Pewter through Viridian Forest and Pallet, then Surf to Cinnabar.
Next: Cinnabar & the Seafoam Islands. East from Cinnabar along Route 20.

11 / site chapter 30: Cinnabar Island — Seafoam & Blaine · Level 65–67
Find Blaine’s temporary Gym in the Seafoam Islands, then meet Blue on Cinnabar.
Next: Viridian Gym: Blue. After speaking to Blue again on Cinnabar, Fly to Viridian.

12 / site chapter 31: Viridian Gym — Blue · Level 67–69 & Professor Oak
Win the sixteenth Badge and report to Professor Oak.
Next: The Kanto League (optional). Fly to the Indigo Plateau for the recommended rematch, or go directly to Mt. Silver after Oak.

13 / site chapter 32: The Kanto League (Optional) — Optional rematch before Mt. Silver
Optionally beat the Elite Four’s stronger teams and Lance before taking on Red.
Next: Mt. Silver. Fly to Viridian, west on Route 22, and through the Reception Gate’s west door.

14 / site chapter 33: Mt. Silver — Route 28 & Red
Climb to the summit and defeat Red.
Next: A Promise to Butterfree. Fly to Viridian and walk north on Route 2 into Viridian Forest.

15 / site chapter 34: A Promise to Butterfree — Ash in Viridian Forest
Help Ash, Misty and Brock find a lost Butterfree, battle Ash and accept his Pikachu. This story is required to reach Hoenn.
Next: The Road to Hoenn. Fly to Pallet Town.

HGSS RESEARCH
HGSS permits flexible Kanto Gym order. The ship arrives in Vermilion; the Power Plant theft/recovery and Misty’s cape encounter drive northern travel. Lavender’s EXPN expansion enables the Poke Flute and western access past Snorlax. Blue requires the seven other Kanto badges.
Project Blonde changes levels/teams, adds Mega Evolution, uses a Radio Upgrade and tuning dial, blocks Route 19 with Kingler until Blaine, and requires Red before Butterfree/Ash/Hoenn. The later Silph Co. story follows the Hoenn League, not this initial Gym circuit.
